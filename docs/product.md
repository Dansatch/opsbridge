# Product brief

OpsBridge is a browser-based miniature warehouse and operations control room. It
should be understandable in under 30 seconds and enjoyable before the visitor has
clicked anything.

## Product promise

Watch a live operation, disturb it, understand what changed and restore the flow.

## First-launch journey

1. Parcels enter receiving and travel through picking, packing and dispatch.
2. Scanners, cameras, handhelds and printers visibly handle the work.
3. A visitor follows an order or inspects a device without leaving the floor.
4. “Cause some trouble” introduces a scanner failure, packing jam or failed label.
5. The warehouse responds visibly and OpsBridge opens an incident automatically.
6. The visitor acknowledges the incident, applies recovery and watches work resume.

## Design rules

- The warehouse floor remains the main attraction.
- Useful information appears in context rather than filling the screen permanently.
- Copy is clear, conversational and lightly playful without hiding operational facts.
- Day and night shifts change atmosphere and order volume.
- Motion explains state; it does not decorate empty space.
- Every metric comes from the same events that drive the simulation.
- All devices, orders and activity are synthetic and original.

## Launch boundaries

The first release has one facility, four workflow stages, one connected route,
three failure scenarios and one contextual detail panel. It does not include user
accounts, multiple sites, configurable floor plans, predictive maintenance or a
general-purpose dashboard builder.
